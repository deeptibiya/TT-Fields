# TTFields Therapy Analysis — Project Plan

## Objective

Conduct a structured, evidence-based analysis of Tumor Treating Fields (TTFields) therapy in glioblastoma, integrating scientific, clinical, regulatory, financial, commercial, and post-launch evidence.

## 24-W roadmap

| Phase | W | Focus |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 1–4 | Discovery & Preclinical Evidence |
| 2 | 5–8 | Clinical Evidence & Regulatory History |
| 3 | 9–12 | Financial & Business Analysis |
| 4 | 13–16 | Commercialization & Launch |
| 5 | 17–24 | Post-Launch Evaluation |

## Phase 1 — Discovery & Preclinical Evidence

**W 1–4**

- Establish the biological rationale for TTFields.
- Review effects on mitosis and cytokinesis.
- Examine microtubules, spindle formation, cell stress, and related cellular mechanisms.
- Review evidence involving DNA damage/repair and other proposed mechanisms.
- Map experimental models used in glioblastoma and other relevant tumor systems.
- Assess evidence quality, reproducibility, limitations, and translational relevance.

## Phase 2 — Clinical Evidence & Regulatory History

**W 5–8**

- Identify pivotal and major clinical studies.
- Extract efficacy outcomes including overall survival and progression-free survival.
- Evaluate quality of life, treatment adherence, and adverse events.
- Compare TTFields with relevant standards of care.
- Build a dated regulatory timeline covering approvals, indications, and major label changes.

## Phase 3 — Financial & Business Analysis

**W 9–12**

- Assess treatment costs and health-economic evidence.
- Review reimbursement and access considerations.
- Examine market size, adoption barriers, and economic value.
- Describe the business model using appropriately sourced evidence.

## Phase 4 — Commercialization & Launch

**W 13–16**

- Evaluate product positioning and clinical adoption.
- Examine implementation, patient experience, treatment logistics, and adherence.
- Assess market access and physician adoption.
- Consider relevant competitive context.

## Phase 5 — Post-Launch Evaluation

**W 17–24**

- Review post-market evidence and real-world data.
- Evaluate longer-term safety, utilization, adherence, and outcomes.
- Track emerging clinical and mechanistic evidence.
- Identify persistent evidence gaps and unanswered questions.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Regulatory and government documents.
2. Randomized controlled trials and prospective clinical studies.
3. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
4. Peer-reviewed mechanistic and preclinical studies.
5. Health-economic and outcomes research.
6. Company materials, used only for company-reported information and clearly identified as such.

## Reproducibility principles

- Trace major conclusions to specific sources.
- Record search dates and relevant inclusion/exclusion decisions.
- Preserve datasets, assumptions, calculations, and analysis code where applicable.
- Clearly distinguish established findings, interpretation, hypotheses, and limitations.
