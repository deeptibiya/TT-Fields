## Key unresolved issue

The FDA S013 Summary of Safety and Effectiveness and the 2012 recurrent-GBM journal publication report different numerical estimates for median overall survival. Both analyses report no statistically significant OS advantage. The estimates are retained separately in the extraction table; analysis populations, data cut-offs, and statistical approaches must be reconciled rather than silently merged.

## Phase 2 priorities

1. Reconcile the recurrent-trial estimate discrepancy against full regulatory and publication materials.
2. Audit endpoint values, confidence intervals, analysis populations, safety denominators, funding, and conflicts of interest.
3. Combine clinical evidence with the [regulatory history](../regulatory/w-06-regulatory-history.md) and [regulatory timeline](../regulatory/regulatory_timeline.csv) in Week 8.

## Evidence standard

Clinical conclusions should distinguish randomized evidence, secondary analyses, observational evidence, and company-reported information. Trial endpoints should retain their time origin, confidence intervals, and analysis population. A compliance subgroup should not be written as a randomized comparison of compliance levels.
