## Key unresolved issue

The FDA S013 Summary of Safety and Effectiveness and the 2012 recurrent-GBM journal publication report different numerical estimates for median overall survival. Both analyses report no statistically significant OS advantage. The estimates are retained separately in the trial-extraction table; analysis populations, data cut-offs, and statistical approaches must be reconciled rather than silently merged.

Other Phase 2 open items include review of exact conflict-of-interest statements, current full US labeling, original historical EU certificate/conformity records, and full Japan/Canada instructions for use.

## Workflow priorities

1. Complete the recurrent-trial estimate reconciliation from the original source documents.
2. Audit endpoint values, confidence intervals, analysis populations, safety denominators, funding, and conflicts of interest.
3. Then move into Phase 3 (Weeks 9–12): health economics, reimbursement, costs, and access burden, keeping independent analyses distinct from company-reported business claims.

## Evidence standard

Clinical conclusions should distinguish randomized evidence, secondary analyses, observational evidence, and company-reported information. Trial endpoints should retain their time origin, confidence intervals, and analysis population. A compliance subgroup should not be written as a randomized comparison of compliance levels.
