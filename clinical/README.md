# Clinical Evidence

## Phase 2 — W 5–8

This directory contains the clinical and regulatory analysis of TTFields in glioblastoma.

### Phase 2 focus

During W 5–8, the project will:

- evaluate pivotal and major clinical studies;
- extract efficacy outcomes including progression-free survival and overall survival;
- assess quality of life, treatment adherence, and adverse events;
- compare TTFields with relevant standards of care; and
- build a dated regulatory history covering approvals, indications, and major label changes.

### Evidence standard

Clinical conclusions should distinguish randomized evidence, secondary analyses, observational evidence, and company-reported information. Key claims should be traceable to the original study or authoritative regulatory source.
