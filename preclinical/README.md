# Preclinical Evidence

## Phase 1 — W 1–4

This directory contains the scientific analysis of TTFields before and around clinical translation.

### Core questions

1. What is the proposed physical and biological mechanism of TTFields?
2. How do alternating electric fields affect mitosis and cytokinesis?
3. What evidence supports effects on microtubules and the mitotic spindle?
4. What cellular stress, DNA-damage, or repair responses have been reported?
5. Which glioblastoma and other tumor models have been studied?
6. How consistent are findings across models and laboratories?
7. Which findings are established versus proposed or still uncertain?
8. How well do preclinical findings translate to human treatment?

### Planned outputs

- Mechanism evidence map
- Preclinical model inventory
- Critical appraisal of key studies
- Evidence gaps and translational limitations
- Public research summaries
