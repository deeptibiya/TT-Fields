# W 11 — Business and Market Context for TTFields

**Project:** Comprehensive Analysis of TTFields Therapy (Optune®) for Glioblastoma  
**Phase:** 3 — Financial & Business Analysis  
**Week:** 11  
**Evidence cut-off:** 17 October 2026  
**Source discipline:** Company-reported operating/financial data are labelled as such; regulatory status is separately checked against FDA records where available.

## Research question

How does NovoCure currently commercialize TTFields, what does its disclosed operating/financial information indicate about adoption and execution, and which market, reimbursement, product-launch, and profitability risks should inform the next phase of this project?

## Executive synthesis

NovoCure is commercializing TTFields through multiple disease-specific devices and treatment settings rather than a GBM-only product story. In its 23 July 2026 Q2 results release, the company reported quarterly net revenue of US$183.6 million (up 16% year over year) and 5,128 active patients on TTFields therapy globally at 30 June 2026. Of these, 4,636 were active on Optune Gio, 207 on Optune Lua, and 285 on Optune Pax. The Q2 release reported US$7.0 million in combined Lua and Pax revenue, including US$5.4 million from Lua and US$1.6 million from Pax. These are company-reported operating metrics, not an independent audit of patient benefit or market demand. [NovoCure Q2 2026 results](https://investor.novocure.com/news-releases/news-release-details/novocure-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results); [SEC Q2 2026 Form 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645113/000164511326000062/nvcr-20260630.htm).

NovoCure's 2025 Form 10-K reported US$655.4 million in 2025 net revenue, and 4,620 total active patients on TTFields at year-end 2025. Its commercial footprint by then included Optune Gio for GBM, Optune Lua for NSCLC/mesothelioma uses in relevant countries, and Optune Pax for pancreatic cancer in the US. FDA records show Optune Pax was approved on 11 February 2026 for adults with locally advanced pancreatic cancer in combination with gemcitabine and nab-paclitaxel; the Q2 2026 release also reported CE marking for the pancreatic-cancer use and Germany as the first EU market to launch. [NovoCure 2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1645113/000164511326000017/nvcr-20251231.htm); [FDA Optune Pax PMA](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P250034).

The commercial model creates a close connection between reimbursement, active patients, time on therapy, monthly billing, consumable-array costs, and cash collection. In the 2025 Form 10-K, the company describes monthly payer billing, negotiated discounts from list prices, and the financial risk of securing payment in markets where it bills payers directly. It also states that active-patient growth depends on both new patient starts and treatment duration. This matters for interpreting revenue growth: revenue can change due to patient counts, geography, reimbursement/contract terms, duration on therapy, product launches, currency effects, and one-time benefits.

## 3. Commercial model and market-access mechanics

NovoCure's 2025 Form 10-K describes a mix of direct-to-patient distribution and hospital-contract models. In its direct-to-payer markets, the company bills a single monthly fee for therapy and bears the financial risk of securing payment from payers and patients. In some markets, including Japan, it distributes through hospitals and provides patient support services under contract. The company also says it maintains a monthly list price but typically negotiates discounts with payers, and sometimes accepts government-mandated discounts to secure reimbursement.

That model has several implications for a GBM-focused business analysis:

1. **Monthly fee is not realized net revenue per patient.** Discounts, payer mix, country mix, free/one-time arrangements, contract structure, duration and collection affect recognized revenue.
2. **Active patients are not new patient starts.** A period-end count is a stock measure; prescriptions, new starts, discontinuations and duration are flows that can move the count.
3. **Time on therapy matters to both revenue and cost.** The company explicitly links active-patient growth to new starts and treatment duration. This must be reconciled with patient benefit, adherence, tolerability and progression rather than treated as a purely financial variable.
4. **Reimbursement remains a growth dependency.** The 2025 Form 10-K reports national reimbursement for Optune Gio in selected markets as of 31 December 2025. It also reports no national reimbursement by that date for Optune Lua's NSCLC/MPM uses or Optune Pax in active markets, while the 2026 Q2 release reports further launches and active patients. The dates differ; use the 2025 status as a dated baseline, not as the definitive status for every market in October 2026.
5. **Coverage and launch are separate.** A device can have FDA authorization or CE marking without automatically obtaining national reimbursement or equal patient access in every country.

## 4. Market opportunity: company estimates versus independent estimates

The 2025 Form 10-K includes company estimates for US addressable populations (for example, estimates of patients potentially eligible for Optune Gio, Lua and Pax). These are useful for understanding management's commercial assumptions, but they are **company-defined opportunity estimates**, not independent epidemiological counts of patients who will receive treatment.

For this project, market sizing needs three separate layers:

- **Disease incidence/prevalence:** independent epidemiological source, with year, geography and disease definition.
- **Clinically eligible population:** applying indication, performance status, treatment line, biomarker/therapy context and other restrictions.
- **Commercially addressable / treated population:** further constrained by awareness, clinician adoption, payer coverage, service capacity, adherence feasibility and actual uptake.

Never label the company's addressable-patient estimate as independent total market size without tracing the definitions and assumptions.

## 5. Commercial risks to track

The company filing and operating model suggest several risk categories to monitor. These are a research checklist, not a claim that every risk has materialized:

- **Concentration risk:** Optune Gio remains the majority of active patients in Q2 2026.
- **Coverage risk:** adoption depends on national reimbursement and private-payer decisions, not only regulatory approval.
- **Pricing/collection risk:** monthly fees and payer-negotiated discounts affect net revenue; direct-to-payer markets expose the company to collection risk.
- **Duration/adherence risk:** active-patient counts and revenue depend on new starts and time on therapy; treatment burden can affect continuity.
- **Launch execution risk:** new products create launch, reimbursement, education and commercial-expense requirements.
- **Supply-chain and manufacturing risk:** arrays, device components, tariffs, suppliers and production efficiency affect cost of revenue.
- **Clinical/regulatory risk:** future indications, including brain-metastasis applications, remain dependent on formal regulatory decisions and trial evidence.
- **Profitability risk:** management's stated path to profitability must be evaluated against GAAP loss, adjusted EBITDA, operating expenses, cash balance and future spending, not revenue growth alone.

The Q2 2026 report, for example, cited a one-time tariff refund that benefited cost of revenue. This illustrates why period-to-period gross-margin movement should not be attributed wholly to structural manufacturing efficiency.

## 6. What this analysis does and does not establish

### Supported by the sources reviewed

- NovoCure reported US$183.6m Q2 2026 net revenue and 5,128 active TTFields patients globally at 30 June 2026.
- Optune Gio represented 4,636 of those active patients; Lua and Pax were smaller but growing commercial product groups in the release.
- NovoCure's commercial model includes monthly payer fees, payer negotiations and different distribution arrangements across markets.
- Optune Pax has an FDA PMA record dated 11 February 2026 for locally advanced pancreatic cancer with gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel.
- The Q2 2026 company release described an FDA decision on TTFields for NSCLC brain metastases as anticipated in Q4 2026, so it must be treated as a pending milestone at that point.

### Not established by this review

- Current net price per patient per month by country.
- A verified current negotiated price for Optune Gio, Lua or Pax.
- Independent market-share or addressable-market estimates.
- Profitability by product or a separate quarterly Optune Gio revenue figure.
- Current reimbursement status in every country as of October 2026.
- Approval of the proposed brain-metastases indication after the Q2 2026 company release; check the FDA record before updating status.

## W 11 takeaway

The company's commercial story combines a mature GBM product, newer applications in other cancers, monthly-fee reimbursement economics and a growing international footprint. Revenue growth and active-patient growth are useful operating metrics, but they are not substitutes for patient-level outcomes, independent market validation, net pricing, or evidence of profitability. Keep company claims, regulatory status, payer access and independent clinical evidence as separate data layers.
