## Interpretation rules

1. An ICER is a model output, not an intrinsic property of the therapy.
2. Do not compare currency per life-year gained directly with currency per QALY.
3. Preserve health system, currency/year, horizon, comparator, discount rate, price assumption and willingness-to-pay threshold.
4. Distinguish list price, sponsor-submitted price, negotiated price, public-payer cost, patient out-of-pocket cost, revenue, and market-size claims.
5. Label company-reported business data as company-reported; do not treat it as independent clinical or economic validation.
6. A clinical guideline, regulatory approval, HTA recommendation, national device licence, and payer coverage rule are different decision types.
7. Do not double-count kit/arrays/support if they are included in a bundled monthly fee.
