## Evidence principles

1. Prioritize primary regulatory records, trial publications, trial registrations, systematic reviews, and peer-reviewed research.
2. Separate regulatory authorization, clinical outcomes, proposed mechanisms, and company-reported claims.
3. Distinguish GBM-specific evidence from evidence in other cancers or animal models.
4. Record study populations, endpoints, effect estimates, limitations, funding and conflicts of interest.
5. Treat a draft as a draft until its important claims and references have been independently checked.

## Scope of the analysis

The project covers scientific mechanisms and preclinical translation; clinical efficacy, safety, quality of life and adherence; regulatory history; then health economics, reimbursement, commercialization and post-launch evidence. Commercial or market claims will be identified by source type and will not be presented as independent clinical evidence.
